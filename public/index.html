How it works

Our family gives to these four charities. Scannibal, the app we make, sends people here too.

  • You give straight to the charity. Your donation goes through Every.org to the charity you pick. It never passes through us.
  • You can see it add up. The total above counts every gift made from this page.

Why it's called the Dia Fund

It's named after our daughter, Dia. She's little now, but we want her to grow up seeing giving as a normal part of life, something you just do.

As she gets older, we hope she'll help choose where it goes.

Where we show up

Three of our four charities are right here in San Francisco and the East Bay, so we can show up in person, not just give online. We also volunteer with Big Brothers Big Sisters of the Bay Area.
