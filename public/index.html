Why we’re doing this

We’re new at this, and it’s small.

We want giving to be a normal part of family life, something Dia sees us do rather than something we talk about. So we picked four causes, found one charity for each, and we’re starting there.

We don’t have it figured out. We’ll share what we learn here as we go.

Scannibal is an app we made. It links here in case people want to give. That’s the only connection.

— Dia’s family

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Every.org is a nonprofit, and they’ll email you a receipt.

Do you take a cut?

No. We never see the money. Every.org takes your gift and sends it to the charity. Their FAQ covers any card fees.

What is Every.org?

A nonprofit that makes giving to any US charity simple. They handle the payment and the paperwork.

Why these four charities?

They’re the causes we want Dia to care about, and these charities put most of every dollar into the actual work. We wrote up how we chose.

How is Scannibal connected?

It’s an app we made. It links here if people want to give. Giving doesn’t unlock anything in the app.

Can I say hi?

Please do. Email hello@thediafund.org.
