The DIA Fund — Dream. Inspire. Awaken.

Named for Dia Gorapalli, The DIA Fund is a family philanthropy dedicated to fighting hunger, protecting animals, safeguarding the environment, and expanding opportunity for underserved children.

Our Mission Pillars

Four commitments that guide our giving and shape a better world.

Hunger Relief

Ensuring no family goes hungry. Supporting food banks and feeding programs that provide nourishing meals to families in crisis.

Animal Welfare

Protecting the animals who share our world. Supporting rescue, rehabilitation, and advocacy organizations.

Environmental Protection

Safeguarding the planet for the next generation. Investing in conservation and sustainability initiatives.

Children's Opportunity

Expanding access to education, health, and a fair start for every child, regardless of circumstance.

How It Works

The DIA Fund channels proceeds from Scannibal and family contributions into direct grants to vetted charitable organizations. We believe in putting resources where they matter most—funding organizations with proven track records and deep community impact.

✓ 100% of donated proceeds go directly to our partner charities

Every dollar flows toward meaningful change: filling empty stomachs, rescuing animals, protecting ecosystems, and opening doors for children.

Growing With Dia

Dia Gorapalli is four years old. The DIA Fund grows with her—a living legacy that evolves from childhood through adulthood. Today, we steward resources on her behalf. Tomorrow, she will lead this fund herself.

This isn't just giving away money. This is building a legacy of compassion from the very beginning. It's teaching that generosity is a responsibility, that impact compounds over time, and that one person—starting young—can shape the future.

The DIA Fund demonstrates that philanthropy isn't just what you do—it's who you become. And Dia's generation will inherit not just wealth, but purpose.
