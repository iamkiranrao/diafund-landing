About us

A family, not a foundation

The Dia Fund is named after our daughter. She's little now, but we want her to grow up seeing giving as a normal part of life, something you just do. As she gets older, we hope she'll help choose where it goes.

Three of our four charities are close to home in San Francisco and the East Bay, so we can show up in person, not just give online. And we’re starting to volunteer with Big Brothers Big Sisters of the Bay Area.

The fourth, WWF, goes back further. One of us got involved with them as a kid, at about Dia's age.

  • Who runs itOur family.
  • Where the money goesStraight to the four charities. Gifts made here never pass through us.
  • How we choseFour-star ratings, most of each dollar spent on the work itself, and a real track record.
  • ScannibalOur family's app. It sends people here if they'd like to give. Nothing in the app depends on it.
FAQ

Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Donations are made through Every.org, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and you'll get a tax receipt from them by email.

Do you take a cut?

No. We never receive or hold any of the money given here. Every.org processes your gift and sends it to the charity you choose. Their FAQ explains any payment processing fees.

What is Every.org?

A nonprofit that makes it easy to give to any US charity. It handles the payment, the receipt, and getting the money to the charity.

Why these four charities?

We wanted one cause each that we'd want Dia to care about, and charities that are highly rated, spend most of each dollar on the work, and have a long track record. Here's how we picked them.

How is Scannibal connected?

Scannibal is an app our family makes. It links here so people can give if they'd like to. Giving doesn't unlock anything in the app.

How do I get in touch?

Email us at hello@thediafund.org.
